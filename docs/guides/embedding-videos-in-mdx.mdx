# Embedding Videos in MDX

Video is a great way to show a workflow that is hard to describe in prose. This guide covers how to embed videos inside MDX documentation pages while keeping them accessible and privacy respecting.

---

## Privacy-respecting embeds

Prefer a player that does not set cookies or track viewers until they click play. Two good options:

- **Self-hosted videos** served from the docs site itself. No third-party requests happen until the video is played.
- **Privacy-enhanced embeds** such as YouTube's `youtube-nocookie.com` domain or Vimeo's `dntly.com` domain. These delay tracking cookies until playback begins.

Avoid plain `www.youtube.com/embed/...` URLs in documentation, because they set advertising cookies as soon as the page loads.

---

## Accessible captions

Every embedded video should have:

1. A closed caption track (`kind="captions"`) in WebVTT (`.vtt`) format, so deaf and hard-of-hearing readers can follow along.
2. A descriptive `title` attribute on the video element, so screen readers announce what the video is about.
3. A short transcript or summary in the surrounding prose, so the information is available even if the video cannot be played.

If you embed from a third party, check that the host provides autogenerated captions and export them as a caption track when possible.

---

## Example

The following example embeds a self-hosted video with captions and a transcript fallback. Place the caption file next to the video in `public/videos/`.

```mxdx
<figure>
<b>Deploying a Nextellar dApp to Vercel</b>
<video controls preload="metadata" width="100%" title="Screen recording of deploying a Nextellar dApp to Vercel">
  <source src="/videos/deploy-to-vercel.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
  <track default kind="captions" srcLang="en" src="/videos/deploy-to-vercel.vtt" label="English" />
  Your browser does not support embedded video. <a href="/videos/deploy-to-vercel.mp4">Download the video</a> instead.
</video>
<figcaption>The clip walks through creating a project, configuring environment variables, and shipping to Vercel.</figcaption>
</figure>
```

To embed a privacy-enhanced YouTube video instead, use an `iframe` with a title and the `nocookie` domain:

```mxdx
<iframe
  width="560"
  height="315"
  src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/DUMMYID?title=0"
  title="Walkthrough of creating a Stellar transaction with Nextellar"
  allow="acceleratormeter; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture"
  allowFullScreen
  loading="lazy"
/>
```

Replace `DUMMYID` with the real video ID and keep the `title` attribute descriptive.

---

## Verifying the example

Before opening a PRr, run the content build and link checker from the repo root:

```bash
pnpm build:content
pnpm check:links
pnpm dev
```

Then open the page in the dev server and confirm:

- The video renders and plays in the browser.
- The caption track is available in the player's captions menu.
- The transcript and download fallback are reachable without the video.
- No third-party cookies are set on page load.
