# Stellar dApp Launch Communications Tutorial

A technically sound launch can still fall flat with no one watching. This tutorial walks through the announcement channels available to Stellar dApp teams, a four-week messaging cadence from teaser to post-launch recap, and copy templates you can adapt for each stage.

## Announcement Channels

Choose channels based on where your target audience already spends time. Most Stellar dApp launches use a combination of the following.

| Channel | Best for | When to post |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| Stellar Community Forum | Core Stellar developers and enthusiasts | T-2 weeks and launch day |
| Stellar Developer Discord | Technical feedback and beta testers | Throughout |
| Twitter/X | Broad crypto audience, journalists | All stages |
| Farcaster | Web3-native early adopters | All stages |
| Email list | Highest intent users, re-engagement | T-1 week and launch day |
| In-app notification | Beta users who already have an account | T-3 days and launch day |
| Product Hunt | General tech audience, first-mover ranking | Launch day |
| Hacker News (Show HN) | Technical audience, bootstrapped/indie projects | Launch day |

### Stellar-specific channels

Post in the `#dapps` and `#showcase` channels on the [Stellar Developer Discord](https://discord.gg/stellar). The Stellar Development Foundation team monitors these channels and may amplify good launches through the [@StellarOrg](https://twitter.com/StellarOrg) account or the developer newsletter. Tag `@StellarDev` when sharing technical milestones.

## Messaging Cadence

### T-4 weeks — Teaser and waitlist

Your goal here is to capture interest and build an email list before you have anything to show. Keep it short.

**What to say:**
- What problem does the dApp solve?
- What makes the Stellar-based approach different?
- Where can people sign up to be notified?

**What to avoid:**
- Over-promising features that are not finished
- Sharing a launch date you are not confident in — delay announcements beat missed dates

### T-2 weeks — Feature preview

Share a short demo video, screenshot thread, or blog post that shows the core user flow. This is your best chance to collect early feedback before the public launch.

**What to say:**
- A concrete "here is what it does in 60 seconds" walkthrough
- One or two standout technical details (e.g., "built on Soroban, settlement in under 5 seconds")
- An invite for beta testers if you have a waitlist

### T-1 week — Reminder and hype

Re-engage everyone who signed up but has not seen recent posts. Count down publicly to build anticipation.

**What to say:**
- "One week to go" message with the launch date
- Testimonial or quote from a beta tester (with permission)
- Reiterate the core value proposition in one sentence

### Launch day

On launch day, post across all channels within a two-hour window. Stagger posts so each platform feels native rather than copy-pasted.

**Sequencing suggestion:**
1. Email your waitlist first (they signed up, they deserve first access)
2. Post in Stellar Discord `#showcase`
3. Publish your launch blog post
4. Tweet the announcement and link the blog post
5. Submit to Product Hunt (noon Eastern is peak traffic)
6. Post on Farcaster

### T+1 week — Lessons-learned post

A short retrospective builds credibility and drives organic discovery. Developers search for "Stellar dApp launch" post-mortems.

**What to include:**
- How many users signed up in the first week
- One thing that went better than expected
- One thing you would do differently
- What comes next

## Copy Templates

### Waitlist tweet

```
We're building [one-sentence description of what the dApp does] on Stellar.

Why Stellar? [One sentence: fast settlement / low fees / Soroban smart contracts / etc.]

Sign up for early access 👇
[waitlist link]

#Stellar #StellarDev #Web3
```

### Stellar Discord announcement (T-2 weeks)

```
Hey Stellar fam 👋 We've been building [dApp name] — [one-sentence description].

Here's a quick demo: [link to video or screenshot thread]

We're looking for beta testers who want to [core action the dApp performs].
DM me or drop your email in this thread if you're interested.

Built on Nextellar + Soroban 🚀
```

### Launch day tweet thread

```
1/ Excited to announce [dApp name] is live on Stellar mainnet 🚀

[One sentence: what it does]
[One sentence: why it matters]

[link to app]

🧵 Here's how it works:

2/ [Screenshot of core flow, step 1]
[Brief caption]

3/ [Screenshot of core flow, step 2]
[Brief caption]

4/ Built on @Stellar + @SorobanSmart, using @NextellarDev for the scaffold.

Transaction finality in ~5 seconds. Fees < $0.01.

5/ Try it now: [link]
Feedback welcome in the replies or our Discord: [link]
```

### Launch blog post outline

```markdown
# We Launched [dApp Name] — Here's What We Built and Why

## The problem
[2–3 sentences describing the pain point]

## Why Stellar
[2–3 sentences: settlement speed, Soroban contracts, low fees, ecosystem]

## How it works
[Short walkthrough with one or two screenshots]

## Technical highlights
- Built with Nextellar CLI
- Smart contracts: Soroban ([language])
- Key design decisions: [e.g., passkey auth, account abstraction]

## What's next
[Two or three upcoming features]

## Try it
[Link to the app]
[Link to the Discord / community]
[Link to GitHub if open-source]
```

### Email to waitlist (launch day)

```
Subject: [dApp name] is live — your early access is ready

Hi [first name or "there"],

It's here. [dApp name] is now live and your early access link is below.

[CTA button: Open [dApp name]]

What you can do right now:
• [Feature 1]
• [Feature 2]
• [Feature 3]

Questions? Reply to this email or join us in Discord: [link]

— [Your name / team]
```
