### Fee auction basics

Under surge pricing, ledger space is allocated by auction:

- **Base fee floor:** every operation must bid at least the network minimum of **100 stroops** (0.00001 XLM). Validators can change this minimum by vote.
- **Higher bids go first.** Transactions are sorted by the fee they bid, and the highest bids fill the ledger.
- **Everyone pays the lowest winning bid.** All included transactions pay the lowest inclusion fee in the set, not their own bid, so a higher bid improves your odds without raising what you pay.
- **Ties are resolved randomly.** If several transactions bid the same fee and not all fit, the network picks among them at random.
- **Losers wait or drop.** Transactions that miss the cut move to the next ledger, or are discarded if they wait too long.

Smart contract transactions compete only with other smart contract transactions for their own share of ledger capacity.
