# Signer Thresholds

This draft for issue #370 accompanies the note in `docs/guides/transaction-lifecycle.mdx`.

### Signer thresholds

Each account sets low, medium, and high thresholds. For each operation, the combined weight of the signers authorizing it must meet or exceed the operation source account's threshold.

- **Low** applies to `Bump Sequence`. The transaction source also needs to meet its low threshold to authorize the envelope.
- **Medium** applies to most operations, including `Payment` and `Change Trust`.
- **High** applies to `Account Merge` and to `Set Options` when changing signer settings or thresholds.

See the [Stellar operation reference](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/learn/fundamentals/transactions/list-of-operations) for the threshold of each operation.
