### Selecting testnet vs. mainnet with the CLI

By default, `npx nextellar` scaffolds projects against **Stellar Testnet**. Pass `--horizon-url` and `--soroban-url` together to target **Mainnet** instead:

```bash
## Testnet (default)
npx nextellar my-testnet-app --defaults

## Mainnet
npx nextellar my-mainnet-app \
  --horizon-url https://horizon.stellar.org \
  --soroban-url https://soroban-rpc.stellar.org
```

**Required environment variables** (written to `.env.example` and configured in `.env.local`):

- `NEXT_PUBLIC_HORIZON_URL` — `https://horizon-testnet.stellar.org` (testnet) or `https://horizon.stellar.org` (mainnet)
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_SOROBAN_URL` — `https://soroban-testnet.stellar.org` (testnet) or `https://soroban-rpc.stellar.org` (mainnet)
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_NETWORK` — `TESTNET` (default) or `PUBLIC` (mainnet passphrase)

<Note type="warning">
  Scaffolded projects default to `TESTNET` to protect against accidental
  real-asset transfers. Before deploying to Mainnet, verify that
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_HORIZON_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SOROBAN_URL`, and
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_NETWORK=PUBLIC` all point to Mainnet—leaving
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_NETWORK` at its `TESTNET` default causes wallet and passphrase
  mismatches, and Mainnet transactions are irreversible and spend real XLM.
</Note>
