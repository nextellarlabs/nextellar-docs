### Anchor Domain

The domain of an organization that operates an anchor on Stellar — for example, `example.com`. Anchors publish a `stellar.toml` file at their domain so wallets and clients can discover their supported assets, SEP endpoints, and issuer accounts. The anchor domain is the trusted root that ties an on-chain asset issuer back to a real-world organization.
