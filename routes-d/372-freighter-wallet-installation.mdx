### Installing Freighter

1. Download the extension from the [official Freighter website](https://www.freighter.app/).
2. Create a new wallet or import an existing recovery phrase, and store the phrase securely offline.
3. Open Freighter settings and switch the network to **Testnet** (matching Nextellar's default) or **Mainnet** to align with your app.

**Browser support:** Freighter is officially supported as a browser extension on **Chrome**, **Firefox**, and Chromium-based browsers such as **Brave** and **Microsoft Edge**.
