# Contributor Spotlight Format

A contributor spotlight introduces a person through the work they chose to share, the problem it addresses, and what they learned. Keep the article focused on the contributor's own perspective, and confirm names, pronouns, quotations, links, and publication consent before publishing.

## Suggested outline

1. **Headline:** Use the contributor's approved name and a specific description of their work.
2. **At a glance:** Role or area of contribution, project or repository, and a link readers can follow.
3. **Introduction:** Briefly describe what they contributed and why it matters to users or maintainers.
4. **The work:** Explain the original problem, the contributor's approach, and one concrete decision or lesson.
5. **Contributor perspective:** Include a short approved quotation or paraphrase from the interview.
6. **Advice and next steps:** Share one practical recommendation and point readers to related work or ways to participate.

Aim for about 500–800 words. A short, specific spotlight is more useful than a full biography; expand only when the technical contribution needs more context.

## Interview prompts

Choose a few prompts rather than asking every question. Invite concrete examples, and let the contributor skip anything they do not want to answer.

- What problem or user need led you to this contribution?
- What part of the work was less obvious than it first appeared?
- Which decision or tradeoff would help another contributor understand the change?
- What did you learn while working on it?
- What would you suggest to someone making their first contribution?
- Is there a person, project, or resource you would like readers to explore next?
- Which name, links, quotation, and personal details are you comfortable having published?

Avoid asking for confidential employer details, private contact information, or stories that imply an endorsement the contributor has not approved.

## Short example

The following is an illustrative placeholder, not a real contributor quote:

> **Contributor:** Example Contributor, wallet accessibility
>
> **What they worked on:** Improved keyboard navigation in a wallet connection flow.
>
> **Why it mattered:** Users could reach and activate connection options without a pointer.
>
> **Lesson:** “I started by testing the flow with only the keyboard. That made the missing focus state clear before I changed the component.”

Replace every placeholder with details reviewed and approved by the featured contributor. Do not invent quotations, impact figures, or attribution.

## Editorial checklist

- Get approval for the final name, pronouns, quotations, and any personal information.
- Link to the contribution and explain its user-facing or maintainer-facing value.
- Keep claims specific and check technical details with the contributor.
- Share the final draft for review before publication.
