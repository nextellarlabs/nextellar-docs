[Albedo](https://albedo.link) is a security-centric, developer-friendly web signer and keystore for Stellar. It is not a traditional full wallet. An app can ask Albedo to sign a transaction or verify identity, and the user's secret key never leaves Albedo.

Use Albedo when someone wants a lightweight signer with no install, or only a browser extension. It fits quick approvals, SSO-style logins, and users who prefer not to install a full extension wallet.
