---
title: Stellar Expert Contract View
description: View Soroban contracts on Stellar Expert
---

Open `https://stellar.expert/explorer/{network}/contract/{contract-id}`, replacing `{network}` with `public` or `testnet` and `{contract-id}` with the full contract address. Contract addresses use the `C`-prefixed Stellar StrKey format; keep the complete address when building the URL.
